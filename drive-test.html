The test estimates your approach using direction and straight-line distance. It has no road route and may miss places or select one beside another road. Poor or old GPS fixes stop automatic playback. A place plays once per session, with a gap between clips.

Test results

Times and place names stay on this phone until you copy them. No continuous GPS track is saved.
