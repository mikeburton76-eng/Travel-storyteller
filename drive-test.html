On the journey

Tap Start journey with sound while parked. A prepared welcome plays first, then GPS starts automatically. If sound needs preparing, the same button changes to Play welcome & start when ready. Allow precise location and keep this page open with the screen awake. A passenger should operate it while you drive. Maps directions take priority; this test cannot detect when Maps is speaking.

Audio preparation
—

Audio prepares further ahead, but entering 500 metres of the saved place coordinates queues the item. The app follows your direction: unplayed items behind you are discarded and closer places ahead take priority. A queued item remains eligible beyond 500 metres only while you are still approaching it. A clip already playing continues beyond 500 metres. All automatic narration needs a suitable direction of travel, including town and village introductions. Once established, the last travel direction is held while stopped and updated when movement resumes. These are straight-line distances to a reference point, not road distances or settlement boundaries; a place set back from the road may not play. It has no road route and may miss places or select one beside another road. Poor or old GPS fixes pause new selection and new playback. Stop or hiding the page clears the journey queue. A place plays at most once per day on this browser, with a gap between clips. Nearby place introductions are checked against their sources annually when used; the app does not independently verify every fact. Statistics retain the source’s census year. Food and accommodation listings within this app still cover Buxton and Tideswell only.
