Journey settings

Tap the large box below to choose a distance. Recommended: 400 m.

Distance is locked while the journey is running. When safely parked, tap Stop above to change it.

Choose how close places should be before their audio can start. Smaller distances suit busy areas; larger distances include more of the surrounding countryside. Places may not be visible from the road.

Choose while parked, before starting. To change it during a journey, stop the journey while safely parked. Audio prepares farther ahead and finishes playing even after you leave this distance.

Your choice is remembered on this phone and browser.

Saved places · Explore or On the Go

Save information for an area before you leave. Read or listen here without GPS, or use the same downloads automatically on a journey. Each area stays saved until you delete it.

Downloads cover selected places near the area, not every place along a route. Photos, external maps and source websites need a connection.

More places take longer and use more storage. Download on Wi-Fi and keep this page open. Fewer may be available in some areas.

Check the saved count before leaving. Add another area by changing the place above. Preparing the same area refreshes it; if interrupted, completed clips and older saved places are kept.

Starting in Tideswell village centre; finishing near the Crescent in Buxton town centre.

    On the journey

    Tap Start journey with sound while parked. Two short notes play first, then GPS starts automatically. Allow precise location and keep this page open with the screen awake. A passenger should operate it while you drive. Maps directions take priority; this test cannot detect when Maps is speaking.

    While connected, up to three audio clips are prepared for places ahead, looking up to 5 km in your direction of travel. Clips download one at a time. A download already started is allowed to finish when the road bends or the nearby list changes; playback still checks that the place is ahead. Only clips marked ready can play through a signal gap while this page stays open. Extra preparation uses mobile data and the existing voice service.

      Audio prepares further ahead, but entering 400 metres of the saved place coordinates queues the item. The app follows your direction: unplayed items behind you are discarded and closer places ahead take priority. A queued clip starts only while you are within 400 metres of its reference point. A clip already playing continues beyond 400 metres. All automatic narration needs a suitable direction of travel, including town and village introductions. Once established, the last travel direction is held while stopped and updated when movement resumes. These are straight-line distances to a reference point, not road distances or settlement boundaries; a place set back from the road may not play. It has no road route and may miss places or select one beside another road. Poor or old GPS fixes pause new selection and new playback. Stop or hiding the page clears the journey queue. A place plays at most once per day on this browser, with a gap between clips. Nearby place introductions are checked against their sources annually when used; the app does not independently verify every fact. Statistics retain the source’s census year. Food and accommodation listings within this app still cover Buxton and Tideswell only.
