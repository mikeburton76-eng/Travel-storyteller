Your driving companion

Hear what makes nearby towns and villages distinctive as you approach: their history, main sights and useful source facts. This test uses Pippa. Arrival introductions work worldwide where source coverage permits.

Buxton test: before you leave

Prepare Buxton on Wi-Fi to save an arrival introduction and up to eight nearby-place clips on this phone. This covers places near Buxton, not your whole driving route.

Before a new test, prepare again on Wi-Fi and preview the audio. Older downloads may need updating to include the current arrival introduction.

Check a saved Pippa clip before leaving:

While parked, play the check or a saved clip through the phone or car output you intend to use. Adjust media volume while it plays. If you cannot hear it, do not start the driving test.

On the journey

First confirm you can hear Pippa. Start while parked. Allow precise location and keep this page open with the screen awake. A passenger should operate it while you drive. Maps directions take priority; this test cannot detect when Maps is speaking.

Arrival introductions use an approximate area around the place, so they can work on winding approaches and without a reliable heading. Individual landmarks still use direction and straight-line distance. It has no road route and may miss places or select one beside another road. Poor or old GPS fixes stop automatic playback. A place plays once per session, with a gap between clips. Nearby place introductions are checked against their sources annually when used; the app does not independently verify every fact. Statistics retain the source’s census year. Food and accommodation listings within this app still cover Buxton and Tideswell only.
