Buxton Crescent is a Grade I listed Georgian landmark designed by John Carr for the fifth Duke of Devonshire. The curved building was the centrepiece of the duke’s plans to develop Buxton as a fashionable spa town. Its original design combined a hotel, an assembly room and five lodging houses. After restoration, the hotel and thermal spa reopened in October 2020.
