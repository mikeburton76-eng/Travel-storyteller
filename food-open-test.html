What this test can tell us

Try places you know. Are the names and locations right, are useful options missing, and do the descriptions help? OpenStreetMap is community-maintained, so entries may be incomplete or out of date.

Photos appear only where a freely reusable image has been matched through the venue’s Wikidata record. They are genuine exterior photographs, sometimes historic, not current food or menu photos. Credits, source and licence are shown. Where there is no suitable image, we show “Photo unavailable”.

Pub listings do not automatically mean food is served. Check the venue’s website, menu and current opening times before travelling. No price, accessibility or allergy suitability is assumed.

No signup, subscription, Google Places connection or live AI request is needed for this trial. Normal app hosting still applies. Future live coverage and AI refreshes would be separate decisions.
