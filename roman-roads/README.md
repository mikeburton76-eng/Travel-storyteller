# Roman road evidence

The numbered JSON files contain a geographic extract of the Itiner-e route segment export retrieved 22 November 2026 from https://www.itiner-e.org/route-segments/download. Attribution: Brughmans, de Soto, Pažout and Bjerregaard Vahlstrup, [Itiner-e](https://itiner-e.org/about), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Rows retain segment ID, original name, certainty and WGS84 line vertices. For this extract all vertices lie between 49.9–59°N and 6°W–1.8°E, the type contains “road”, and the author field includes Joseph Lewis, excluding continental segments in the bounding box. Bands group by the first vertex latitude, clamped to 50–55. These 1,427 research segments may represent multiple sections of one road.

The separately reviewed records carry sources for the present-day status of individual sections. Driving lines near Smalldale and High Cross are short extracts of modern road geometry, © OpenStreetMap contributors ([ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)), obtained via FOSSGIS OSRM and checked against the historical references in reviewed.json. Their endpoints limit the GPS feature. Atlas geometry alone does not establish modern carriageway reuse or public access.
