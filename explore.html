About Discover Places

Learn about the places around you and find ideas for what to see and do, with stories where they add interest.

Why we built it

Discover Places began as a driving buddy: a way to hear about interesting places along a journey. It grew into a companion for exploring nearby or discovering somewhere new from home.

How we find the information

AI uses digital sources, including reference articles and travel guides, to prepare information and suggestions. We check for updates monthly when a guide is in use, refreshing from those sources rather than relying only on fixed scripts. Source links and check times are shown where available.

A source check does not independently verify every fact. Check opening times, access, prices and event dates with the venue or organiser. AI can make mistakes; please report anything that needs attention.

Using it on a journey

Set up before driving and stop somewhere safe before interacting. Use audio alongside Google Maps, Apple Maps or another navigation app; navigation instructions and road safety always take priority. We cannot guarantee automatic pausing for another app’s directions or playback with the screen locked.

In this version, location finds nearby suggestions and you choose what to hear. Continuous route narration is not enabled on this page. A separate driving test can prepare audio ahead and test automatic playback, including downloaded clips without signal.
